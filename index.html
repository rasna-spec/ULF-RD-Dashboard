Automated prosthetic test rig

Build a benchtop rig that pulls the prosthetic tendon cable, measures input force, measures grip force, logs data, and helps decide if a printed hand is ready for field testing.

Construir uma bancada de teste que puxa o cabo/tendão da prótese, mede a força de entrada, mede a força de preensão, registra dados e ajuda a decidir se a mão impressa está pronta para testes.

Essential scope

AudienceUniversity engineering team
OutputBuild file + test bench package
StatusDraft for external handoff

Purpose

Test a 3D-printed body-powered hand before clinical or field use.

Why it matters

Static grip checks are not enough. The rig must show force, fatigue, slip, creep, and failure points.

Keep simple

Build only what gives useful test data. Avoid overcomplicated sensors or fixtures.

Linear actuatorPulls the tendon cable in a controlled repeatable motion.
Cable load cellMeasures input pull force going into the Bowden / tendon cable.
Wrist mountHolds the printed hand securely during testing.
Grip target sensorMeasures force applied by the hand to the object or fixture.

SafetyLimit switch, emergency stop, and overload stop.

Force transfer

Compare cable pull force to grip force.

Fatigue

Repeat open / close cycles and track force loss or cracks.

Slip

Pull objects out of the grasp and record slip force.

Creep

Hold the grasp and measure force decay over time.

Off-axis load

Check side loads, twist, and weak print-layer failure.

Environment

Check heat, friction, and material wear where possible.
