Engineering rule

process

Keep this simple: build, test, improve. Do not move to the next stage until the current stage meets the requirement.

Step
What happens
Output
Gate
01

Research

Understand need

Gather user need, clinical constraints, object/task requirements.

Design inputs

Requirements and measurable targets.

Review

Can the team design against this?

02

Concept

Choose architecture

Sketch and compare simple mechanical options.

Concept package

Selected concept + rejected options.

Approve

Move to CAD only after approval.

03

CAD + Prototype

Build testable version

CAD, print, assemble, and document changes.

Prototype

Prototype + test setup.

Test

Pass/fail evidence.

04

Improve

Repeat

Fix failures and retest until requirements are met.

Revision log

What changed and why.

Release

Only after review.
